After finals, learning to live abundantly again

Jan 16, 2026
Today, the weight of finals and endless tasks has finally lifted. My sleep has been a chaotic blur lately—restless and sparse. Around 9 PM, a wave of malaise drove me to bed, where I lingered in that hollow space between wakefulness and dreams. By 11:56 PM, I found myself fully awake, yet deeply fatigued. I began listening to 'The Shorekeeper’s Theme.' It is remarkably soothing, yet it carries a defiant, positive undertone: I want to play the piano, I want to train, I want to live—truly live—for myself. I crave a life of warmth and intensity; I refuse to be just another exam robot. We are meant to live lives of abundance; even when the world bears down on us, we must not allow our spirits to wither. In the embrace of the piano, my mind finally exhales. My soul drifts back to a carefree past, where beautiful moments flicker like scenes from a vintage film. I am profoundly moved.
